# Runbook 30.4: Stuck Claim Deadline Job Recovery

> **Severity:** High (Sev-2) — Financial Risk (Statute of limitations expiration)  
> **Reference:** Master Specification §11 (Scenario 8 & 24), §18, §30.4, §24 Phase 10 & 16  
> **Target Job:** `MissedDeadlineScanJob` / `SlaBreachScanJob` (Quartz.NET in `ResolveOps.Worker`)

---

## 1. Overview & Triggers

Carrier claims have strict legal deadlines (e.g. 9 months under Carmack Amendment, 60 days for concealed damage, 14 days under Montreal Convention).
The Quartz.NET background job `MissedDeadlineScanJob` runs every 15 minutes to evaluate upcoming claim filing deadlines and mark expired claims as `Lapsed` or generate high-priority escalation notifications.
If Quartz.NET stalls, crashes, or suffers thread starvation, deadlines may pass unnoticed without carrier filing.

### Alert Triggers
- **Seq / Health Check Alert:** `QuartzJobHeartbeatMissed` for `MissedDeadlineScanJob` (No execution in > 45 minutes).
- **Metric:** `resolveops.claims.deadlines.scan.latency > 1800s`.

---

## 2. Diagnostics & Scheduler Inspection

### Step 2.1: Check Quartz Scheduler Health in Worker Logs
In Seq or log console:
```text
@Message like "%MissedDeadlineScanJob%" or SourceContext = "Quartz.Core.QuartzScheduler"
```
Look for:
- `JobExecutionException`
- `SchedulerException: Job died`
- Database lock timeouts on Quartz tables (`QRTZ_FIRED_TRIGGERS` or `QRTZ_LOCKS` if clustered).

### Step 2.2: Identify Claims Near Deadline
Query active claims nearing deadline that may have been missed:
```sql
SELECT
    c.id AS claim_id,
    c.tenant_id,
    c.claim_number,
    c.status,
    c.deadline_utc,
    DATEDIFF(HOUR, SYSUTCDATETIME(), c.deadline_utc) AS hours_remaining
FROM claims c WITH (NOLOCK)
WHERE c.status IN ('Draft', 'PendingApproval')
  AND c.deadline_utc <= DATEADD(DAY, 2, SYSUTCDATETIME())
ORDER BY c.deadline_utc ASC;
```

---

## 3. Manual Recovery & Remediation Procedure

### Step 3.1: Trigger Bounded Manual Recovery Scan
If the Worker process is stalled:
1. Restart the `ResolveOps.Worker` process:
   ```bash
   # In Docker / Compose
   docker restart resolveops-worker
   ```
2. Alternatively, invoke the manual administrative trigger endpoint (restricted to Operations Admin):
   ```http
   POST /api/admin/jobs/claim-deadlines/run-now
   Authorization: Bearer <AdminToken>
   ```

### Step 3.2: Verify Idempotent Notification Generation
Because claim deadline notifications generate deterministic notification IDs based on `tenant_id`, `claim_id`, and `deadline_tier` (e.g. `CLM-DUE-7D`, `CLM-DUE-24H`), running a manual scan will NOT send duplicate emails or in-app alerts for already-warned deadlines.

---

## 4. Verification & Prevention

1. Confirm `MissedDeadlineScanJob` logs execution completion:
   ```text
   "MissedDeadlineScanJob: evaluated {Count} claims across {TenantCount} tenants in {ElapsedMs}ms."
   ```
2. Verify claims past deadline transitioned to `Lapsed` with audit trail:
   ```sql
   SELECT id, status, updated_at_utc, updated_by
   FROM claims
   WHERE status = 'Lapsed'
     AND updated_at_utc >= DATEADD(HOUR, -1, SYSUTCDATETIME());
   ```
3. Confirm alerts cleared in Seq monitoring dashboard.
